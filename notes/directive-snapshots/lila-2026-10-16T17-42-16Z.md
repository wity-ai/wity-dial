=== HOW THINGS ARE BUILT HERE ===
- Arrange the video with the track commands: sequence.reorder / sequence.move (order), sequence.gap.set (a pause), sequence.shift (nudge one clip, the others stay), sequence.resize (length), sequence.transition.set (how a clip comes in from the one before).
- Titles and graphics go on a free layer: layer.add, then element.add with a begin and a dur.
- The video's shape is the scene's size: scene.set with a ratio (it is also what new segments are generated in).
- Several clips in the frame at once: layout.apply.
- Clips come from segments: propose add-segment. When a segment's media is ready its clip is put in its track — you never place media yourself.
- add-segment's outcome says what happened: queued (generation started), ready (with the segment's id and its media), or timeout.
- When the context is not enough, read first: scene.read.
- A refused action changed nothing: its outcome says what to fix.
